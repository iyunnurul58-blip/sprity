Sprity.

“Declutter with purpose, Shop as Opportunity”

Sprity is a digital opportunity shop, a platform that turns donated preloved goods into social impact. We collect unused clothing, sanitize and verify every piece, and resell it through a curated catalogue to transparently fund partner charities.

OUR CORE VALUES

Why Choose Sprity?

Seamless Donation

Donate without boundaries or complex procedures. Freely choose between Pick Up (Jakarta area) or Drop Off delivery.

Verified Product

Every preloved item undergoes strict Quality Control (QC) inspection and hygienic sanitization before catalogue listing.

Social Impact with Transparency

Net profits are transparently disbursed to charity partners, backed by public audit reports.
